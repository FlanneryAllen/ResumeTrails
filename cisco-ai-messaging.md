# Cisco AI Messaging Framework

When ChatGPT landed, Cisco had 20+ business units and dozens of executives saying different things about AI. I built the messaging framework that kept the company coherent.

Shifted the conversation from “what AI is” to “what AI does for humans.” The framework became the foundation for Cisco’s AI Readiness Index and cisco.com/ai. It still runs without me.
